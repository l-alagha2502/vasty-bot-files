Policy

Last Updated: March 31, 2026  |  Vasty Bot  |  Vast™

1. Data Collection

To function properly, Vasty Bot collects and stores the following data:

  • ID User & Server IDs: To track leveling (XP), Sparks, and permissions.
  • META Message Metadata: To calculate server-wide stats and execute the media-only/counting game logic.
  • SPEC Vast Card Specs: All 14 data points (social links, PFP, location, etc.) provided during the customization process are stored for manual fulfillment.
  • IMG Attachments: Images sent in the "Proof of Work" channel are processed to validate economy rewards.
2. How Data is Used

Data is used strictly to provide the bot's features.

We do not sell your data. We do not track your activity outside of the servers where Vasty Bot is present.
3. Data Retention
  • Active Data: Stored as long as you are active in a server with the bot.
  • Ticket Data: Support ticket transcripts are stored for 30 days for quality assurance and then deleted.
  • Deletion: You may request a complete wipe of your personal data (XP, Sparks, and Card Specs) by opening a support ticket via the bot.
4. Security

Your data is stored in a secured MongoDB database. Only the Vast™ Founder and authorized developers have access to the backend systems.

5. Governing Law

These policies are governed by the laws of the State of Qatar, where Vast™ is headquartered.
